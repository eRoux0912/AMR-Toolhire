About AMR Toolhire

AMR Tool Hire is a St Helena Bay-based tool hire company. We proudly serve St Helena Bay and also assist customers in Vredenburg, Velddrif, Paternoster, and surrounding areas — we provide reliable, well-maintained, job-ready equipment for construction, demolition, concrete breaking, drilling, earthworks, and DIY projects, backed by a workshop that takes both customer service and equipment care seriously. We also offer specialised chemical rock-breaking and drilling services, providing a safe, blast-free solution for precise rock removal.
